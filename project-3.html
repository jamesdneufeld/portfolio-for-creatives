Stars of the Seasons

A local farm-to-table brand offering frozen, seasonal produce grown and packaged at peak freshness. The visual identity uses constellations to represent how the stars reflect seasonal and weather patterns, connecting this natural cycle to the brand’s promise of preserving each harvest’s nutrients and flavours at the moment of picking. Each constellation was given a unique identity and story, inspired by how ancient civilizations used star patterns to track seasons, guide agricultural activities, and explain natural phenomena. This historical connection reinforces the brand’s focus on seasonal, sustainable farming and its commitment to preserving resources for future generations.
