The Playground page

A Playground page is often a scattered grid of half-finished projects, random ideas, personal experiments, and rejected work we don’t usually get to see in a polished portfolio. It’s the space where the rules don’t apply—you don’t need to fit a brief, explain your process, or curate what stays and what goes.

This is the low-pressure zone.

If you’re feeling unsure about sharing your work, start with a Play page.

It’s a space for dreams and ideas still finding their footing.

Want to play with JavaScript?

This version takes the simple CSS rotation from the Playground page and adds a little JavaScript. Each time you hover over an image, JavaScript randomly chooses a rotation from a small set of angles. Move your mouse away and the image returns to its original position.

This experiment was inspired by a suggestion from Bachelor of Design student Benjamin Kimberley.

Compare the two demos to see what CSS can do on its own and how JavaScript can add a little extra behaviour.
