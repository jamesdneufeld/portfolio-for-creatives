Create a custom error page that reflects your personal brand.

The best 404 pages are an art form in themselves. Clever 404 pages can act like Easter eggs: hidden treats buried in a site. That feeling of discovering something unexpected can turn a wrong turn into a memorable experience and help overcome the frustration of not finding the page you wanted. Use your own personality, interests, visual style and sense of humour to make the page feel like an extension of your portfolio, not a generic error message.
