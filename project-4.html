Tip The Scales

A travelling exhibition at reptile shelters that teaches middle school children and up the complexity and work that goes into keep reptiles as pets. It is done through the style of 1920s scientific field expedition to make what would be a tedious task of looking up information into an adventure that makes children understand the animal as a explorer discovering a new species for the first time.
