Roarchestra Music Festival Posters & Collateral

A series of three posters and two examples of collateral promoting music festivals all over Japan themed around the soundtrack of various kaiju movies. Modelled after classic kaiju movie posters to capture the retro, but classic feeling of these movies not only ensures the idea of the festival is captured, but also making sure the fun spectacle is still clear from the posters.
