Project Prehistory

A mentorship project that reimagines the American Museum of Natural History’s Fossil Hall as a more immersive, engaging, and accessible experience for younger audiences. The concept was driven by the idea that museums should do more than display artifacts; they should spark curiosity, encourage exploration, and make visitors feel actively involved in the learning process. Expedition Prehistory achieves this through a more playful visual identity, combining stylized prehistoric illustrations, bold and colourful typography, and a vibrant palette to create a stronger sense of discovery.

A central mascot, Alex the Tiktaalik, acts as a guide throughout the hall, addressing visitors directly, posing thought-provoking questions, and introducing scientific concepts in an approachable way. Rather than relying solely on conventional informational signage, the project uses visual storytelling, interactive prompts, and a sense of adventure to encourage younger visitors to engage with the exhibits, ask questions, and develop a deeper appreciation for paleontology.
