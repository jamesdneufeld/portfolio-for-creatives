Project Prehistory

Mentorship project where the American Museum of Natural History Fossil Hall is reimagined to be more immersive and engaging for a younger demographic.By adding stylized illustrations, more punchy, colourful typography, pallette and a mascot guiding users throughout the hall asking questions and addressing people directly, Expedition Prehistory ensures the learning experience is more engaging and thought provoking.
